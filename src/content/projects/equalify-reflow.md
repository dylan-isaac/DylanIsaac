problem: "Higher education teams need to make large bodies of course material usable without treating every supported document as a slow, one-off manual remediation project."
approach: "Designed a semantic transformation workflow: document extraction, narrow AI procedures, human review checkpoints, PII safeguards, known-limitation boundaries, and auditable outputs."
impact: "For supported document types, the workflow reduced remediation cost from roughly $9/page to about $0.09/page while keeping human review in the loop."
