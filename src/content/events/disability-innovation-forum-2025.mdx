## 🌟 About the Event

The Disability Innovation Forum brings together investors, nonprofit advocates, business leaders, and entrepreneurs with and without disabilities to explore how technology is transforming education, skills training, and work for people with disabilities.

Hosted by **Enable Ventures**, **SmartJob**, and **Strada Education Foundation**, this premier event showcases innovative solutions that create economic mobility and workforce inclusion.

### 🏆 Event Highlights

- **Big Ideas Showcase**: Five startup founders present groundbreaking disability tech solutions
- **AI and Accessibility**: Exploring AI's potential for disability support in education and workplaces
- **Investment Opportunities**: Investors discuss workforce inclusion technologies
- **Corporate Leadership**: Accessibility leaders from global companies share insights
- **Disability Tech Entrepreneurship**: Art, innovation, and storytelling in disability tech
